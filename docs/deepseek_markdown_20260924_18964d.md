# Quick Cash — Investment Opportunity Cards

## Game Rules Snapshot
- **Starting cash:** $100,000 per player
- **Goal:** First to reach **$500,000** (“the Moon”)
- **Match length:** Up to 6 rounds
- **Round length:** 15 seconds
- **Betting style:** All-in — every choice commits 100% of current cash
- **Inactivity penalty:** -3% on current cash

## Risk Tier Rules
| Tier | Colour | Return Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Safe** | Green | Guaranteed small positive return |
| **Medium** | Amber | Guaranteed moderate positive return |
| **High Risk** | Red | 50/50 gamble: big win or heavy loss |

**Return Ranges**
- **Safe:** +6% to +15%
- **Medium:** +18% to +35%
- **High Risk:** Win **+70% to +120%** / Lose **-35% to -60%**

All returns are applied to the player’s **entire cash balance**.

# Quick Balance Check
- **Safe-only path:** $100K → ~$177K after 6 rounds (not enough to win)
- **Medium-only path:** $100K → ~$380K after 6 rounds (close but not guaranteed)
- **Mixed path with High Risk wins:** can exceed $500K by Round 4–6
- **High Risk losses:** can drop a player below $50K, creating comeback drama

These values keep the game fast, force risk-taking, and preserve the **“first to $500K”** tension.
