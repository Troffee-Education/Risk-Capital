# Level 1 — Real-World Investment Scenario Bank

Each round presents **one real-world investment opportunity**. Players evaluate:
1. **The Business / Opportunity**: What the business actually does in clear, relatable language.
2. **The Risk Score (out of 10)**: Clear risk grading with realistic win rates and real downside loss risks.
3. **The Investment Slider (0% – 100%)**: Players choose how much cash to risk. Any uninvested cash stays 100% safe in bank reserves.
